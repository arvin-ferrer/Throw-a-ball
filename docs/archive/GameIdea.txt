 Game Idea Roblox 



- Every 5 mins a boss will spawn in the arena, and once defeated they drop an egg.
- Training Range to grind their attributes, basically players just need to shoot some target to increase their throwable ball damage. The player should be stationary to shoot their ball.

- Once the boss is defeated it will spawn an egg that is based on the difficulty of the boss.
